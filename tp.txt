NGINX SETUP INFO:

sure, imagine a restaurant kitchen.
right now your setup:
one chef (dataserver) cooking all the food (price analytics), one waiter (clientserver) serving it to customers (WebSocket clients). works fine but if the chef gets sick, no food. if the waiter gets sick, no service.

what we're building:
two waiters (clientservers)
both know the full menu, both can serve any customer. nginx is the host at the door — when a customer walks in, host sends them to whichever waiter is less busy. if waiter-1 gets sick, host sends everyone to waiter-2. customers don't even notice.
two chefs (dataservers) — but only one cooks at a time
here's the key part. if both chefs cook the same dish simultaneously, customers get double portions (duplicate analytics). so we use a golden spatula rule:

only the chef holding the golden spatula is allowed to cook
chef-1 grabs the spatula on startup and starts cooking
chef-2 stands by watching, trying to grab the spatula every few seconds
chef-1 must wave the spatula every 3 seconds to prove he's still alive
if chef-1 collapses and stops waving → spatula drops → after 10 seconds chef-2 grabs it and starts cooking
customers (via the waiters) barely notice the gap

the golden spatula = a Redis key with a timer
that's literally all leader election is. SET leader chef1 NX EX 10 = "put my name on the spatula, auto-drop it in 10 seconds unless I renew"

DR (disaster recovery) story:

waiter dies → nginx stops sending customers there, other waiter handles all of them
chef dies → standby chef picks up the spatula in ~10 seconds, resumes cooking
Redis dies → both chefs lose the spatula, both stop cooking (this is the next phase you mentioned — but for now Redis is a single point of failure we accept)
