Push vs Pull like you're 10:
Imagine a newspaper delivery guy (the hub) and you're a subscriber (client).
Push (Send channel):
The delivery guy throws every single newspaper at your door every morning — Times of India, Economic Times, Sports, Local news, everything — whether you want it or not. Your job is to pick up the pile and find the one you actually wanted. If the pile gets too big before you pick it up, papers start falling off (drops).
Pull (IndicatorFeed channel):
You call the delivery guy and say "I only want Sports on Tuesday and Business on Friday." He now only delivers exactly that. Your doorstep never gets cluttered.

Why pull has better latency — and you're right, it's NOT the 256 vs 2048 thing.
The buffer size just affects how many messages can queue up before dropping. It doesn't make delivery faster.
Pull latency is better because of less competition. In push, 100 clients all joined 10 rooms, so when the SMA ticker fires — 20 instruments × 100 clients = 2000 messages get shoved into channels simultaneously. Everyone's waiting in line.
In pull, a client subscribed to sma:101 only — only that one topic's messages go into its IndicatorFeed. Shorter queue, less waiting, faster delivery. It's like a dedicated express lane vs a crowded highway.
